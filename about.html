OUR PURPOSE

Technology should feel more useful, not more complicated.

SmartTech Guide is a growing collection of simple browser-based tools and approachable technology guides.

What we aim to do

We want to help people finish common digital tasks with less friction. That means making everyday image tasks straightforward, explaining technology in plain English, and helping readers evaluate useful AI services without hype.

How this website works

  • Useful tools: Browser-based image features run on your device where supported, so selected image files do not need to be uploaded to our server.
  • Practical guides: Articles focus on clear steps, use cases and meaningful limitations rather than inflated claims.
  • Honest information: Third-party tools can change their features and pricing. Always check the provider's current terms before relying on a service.

How we improve

This is an independent project and the site may change as we test tools and receive feedback. If you find an error, confusing instruction or broken feature, please report it through our public project repository.
