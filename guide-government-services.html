DIGITAL SERVICES · STARTER MAP

Find the right official portal before you apply.

Use this map to start with established government platforms, then verify the exact eligibility, documents, fees and process for your task.

1. Digital documents: DigiLocker

DigiLocker is the Government of India's digital document wallet initiative. It is designed to let users access, share and verify documents issued through participating organisations.

  1. Open the official domain and review the sign-in or sign-up instructions.
  2. Use only your own account and the details requested on the official site.
  3. For a document to appear, check whether the relevant issuing organisation and document type are available.
  4. Do not share your OTP or security PIN with anyone claiming they can “activate” the service for you.

2. Government schemes: myScheme

myScheme is a national portal for discovering government schemes and reviewing information such as eligibility and application guidance.

  1. Choose the relevant category or search for the scheme.
  2. Read eligibility criteria carefully, including age, income, state and other applicable conditions.
  3. Prepare documents only after checking the scheme's official requirements.
  4. Follow the application path provided by the portal and confirm details with the administering department.

3. Jobs and career support: National Career Service

National Career Service is a Government of India platform for job search and employment-related services. NCS states that its services do not charge fees at any stage of registration, job application or interview processing. Be cautious if someone falsely claims to represent NCS and asks for payment.

4. Suspected cybercrime

Visit the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal for reporting and guidance. For suspected online financial fraud in India, call 1930 promptly and contact your bank through its official channel.

5. Government-related viral claims: PIB Fact Check

The Press Information Bureau (PIB) Fact Check portal provides a channel for checking suspicious information about the Government of India. It is not a universal fact-checker for every claim; for other topics, also review the original source and independent reliable reporting.

How to avoid fake portals

  • Check the whole domain name, including spelling and unexpected extra words.
  • Be suspicious of advertisements or messages promising guaranteed approval for a fee.
  • Never hand over an OTP, password or PIN to an agent.
  • Find the service independently instead of opening unexpected message links.
  • Read the terms, eligibility and official fee information before paying anything.
