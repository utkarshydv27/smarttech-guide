ALWAYS REMEMBER

A real-looking logo is not proof.

Scammers can imitate a bank, government office, courier, employer or person you know. Verify through a contact method you find independently.

!

Never share one-time codes

Do not disclose OTPs, account passwords, card PINs or UPI PINs to callers, chat messages or unknown websites.

IF MONEY WAS LOST

Act promptly.

For suspected online financial fraud in India, call 1930 promptly and use the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.

What this checker can — and cannot — do

It can highlight clues

Some common phrases linked to urgent threats, requests for secrets, suspicious links, upfront fees or unexpected prizes.

It cannot prove a message is safe

It is a simple pattern-based checklist, not an AI system, antivirus, link scanner or official fraud determination. A message with no detected clues may still be dangerous.

Verify and report independently

Do not click the link in the message. Open an official website yourself, contact the organisation through a known channel, or report cybercrime through the official portal.
