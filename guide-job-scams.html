Looking for a job? Learn how to spot fake offers.

A simple checklist for verifying recruiters, job postings and requests for payment before you share information.

Warning signs to take seriously

  • Upfront payments: a recruiter asks you to pay registration, training, security, equipment or interview fees to secure a job.
  • Guaranteed selection: someone promises a job without a meaningful interview or assessment.
  • Unverifiable identity: the email domain, company website, job title or recruiter identity does not match the organisation's independently listed details.
  • Pressure: you must pay immediately, move the conversation to an unfamiliar channel or keep the offer secret.
  • Sensitive information too early: the sender asks for passwords, OTPs, bank login details or identity documents before the employer and process are verified.
  • Unrealistic earnings: a simple task supposedly guarantees high income, particularly if you must deposit money first.

How to verify a recruiter

  1. Find the company website independently; do not rely only on a link supplied by the sender.
  2. Compare the job title, location, responsibilities and contact details with the official careers page.
  3. Contact the organisation using a phone number or email listed on its own official website.
  4. Search for the exact job title and recruiter name, but treat online reviews as clues—not proof.
  5. Read any contract, salary details, working hours, probation terms and deductions before accepting.
  6. Never let someone use your bank account or payment wallet to receive and transfer funds on their behalf.

Use a government career resource

The Government of India's National Career Service (NCS) offers employment-related services. NCS states that its services are free at all stages of registration, job application and interview processing, and warns people about entities falsely claiming an association. Always check the genuine ncs.gov.in domain.

What if someone already asked you to pay?

Stop before sending money. Independently verify the employer and the reason for the fee. Save the posting and messages. If you suspect cyber fraud or have lost money, contact your bank through its official channel and report promptly at the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal; India’s cyber financial fraud helpline is 1930.

Remember: A professional-looking offer letter, logo, social profile or website can be copied. Verify the actual organisation and its hiring process.

This guide is educational. Hiring practices vary; confirm any fees, eligibility, deadlines and application rules with the employer's verified official channel.
