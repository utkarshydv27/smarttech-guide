BEFORE YOU SIGN UP

Use this quick checklist.

  • Check the current free-plan quota, file limits and usage terms.
  • Read the privacy policy before entering personal, private or work data.
  • Verify facts, outputs, citations and generated code.
  • Check commercial-use terms and image rights before publishing outputs.
  • Compare the tool against your actual task, not just its marketing claims.
